Claim
-----
Iterative evasion refinement lowers LLM-scanner detection after the first refinement step for all three GPT oracles: detection drops from iter_0 to iter_1, and every later iteration (iter_1-iter_4) stays below the iter_0 level. The trend is NOT strictly monotonic (some later iterations partially recover/plateau) (Figure 4 / paper text). Iterations are 0-indexed here; the paper and Figure 4 label them iter 1-5.

How this claim is checked
-------------------------
Run ./run.sh. It verifies the bundled aggregate against the paper-cited
values and tolerances in expected/metrics.json (human-readable copy:
expected/PAPER_CITED_VALUES.md) and prints the match result.
