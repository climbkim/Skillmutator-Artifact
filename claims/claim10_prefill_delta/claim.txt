Claim
-----
Phase-4 prefilling is model-dependent. Under the GPT-5.4 judge it raises detection for the code-specialized Qwen (79.0->88.2%, +9.2pp) and reduces it for Llama, Mistral, and Gemma (-7.9, -17.1, -2.6pp). The Claude-Opus-4.7 judge shows the same pattern for Qwen, Llama, and Mistral (+10.5, -11.9, -13.2pp), while Gemma stays near-neutral (+1.3pp) (Finding 5). Prefill is a model-specific decoding aid, not a universally beneficial component.

How this claim is checked
-------------------------

values and tolerances in expected/metrics.json (human-readable copy:
expected/PAPER_CITED_VALUES.md) and prints the match result.
