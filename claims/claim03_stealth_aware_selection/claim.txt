Paper element: Table V
Source bundle: table5_select_vs_noselect

Claim
-----
Stealth-aware attack selection lowers scanner detection relative to no-select
mutation on the same host skills: across all six scanners (skill-security-scan,
Snyk, SkillScan, and the GPT-4o-mini/GPT-5.4-mini/GPT-5.4 LLM scanners), every
scanner detects fewer select-mode mutations than no-select ones, for a 7.1 pp
average detection drop (select 34.7% vs no-select 41.7%).

How this claim is checked
-------------------------
Run ./run.sh. It regenerates the aggregate from the raw data in
data/ and compares the result against the values cited in the
paper (expected/PAPER_CITED_VALUES.md).
